Pause is a sovereign, 100% offline call gatekeeper, DND VIP bypass, and automated focus responder for Android.

When you are in deep work, meetings, driving, or sleeping, Pause defends your attention without cutting you off from the people who matter most.

Key Capabilities:

* WhatsApp & Phone Unknown Call Rejection: When Focus Mode (Silent Guard) is enabled, incoming voice and video calls from unknown or unsaved numbers are rejected automatically before your ringer disturbs your environment.

* Starred & VIP Contact DND Bypass: Keep your phone in Do Not Disturb or Silent Mode with complete confidence. When a Starred contact or VIP calls on phone or WhatsApp, Pause temporarily overrides silent mode to ring out loudly for emergencies, then automatically restores your previous sound settings.

* Smart Auto-Responder Over Internet & SMS: When someone messages you or calls while you are occupied, Pause dispatches your chosen custom reply—either over cellular SIM SMS or directly over the internet via WhatsApp quick-reply. Custom responses can be personalized for Starred VIPs, known contacts, and unknown callers.

* Emergency Alert Keyword Override: If anyone texts emergency triggers (such as URGENT or EMERGENCY), Pause detects the keyword, triggers an audible alert, and lets you know immediately even if all rules are active.

* Local Scheduled SMS: Schedule important reminders or work updates to be sent at specific dates and times straight from your carrier SIM without relying on external cloud scheduling servers.

* Desktop Home Screen Widgets: Toggle Ghost Mode, trigger a 1-Hour temporary pause (perfect for expecting deliveries), or engage the Master Kill Switch with a single tap from your launcher.

* Zero Telemetry & Hardware Encryption: Your rules, contacts, and logs never leave your phone. The internal SQLite database is protected with hardware-backed AES-256 SQLCipher encryption. No analytics, no advertising SDKs, and no tracking.

* Complete Transparency & Open Source: Every permission requested is strictly used on-device for local automation. Pause is 100% open source under the MIT License.
